4xD 0.2.2 beta

A newer version is available: 4xD 0.3.0 beta, with undo/redo and band bypass.

Edge editing

  • Manual Edge edits enforce a half-octave minimum spacing from neighbouring edges.
  • Dragging, arrow controls, double-click reset and numeric entry use the same spacing rule.
  • Only the edited edge moves.
  • Existing presets, sessions and automation are preserved.

Installers

Mac and Windows installers include the licence and third-party notices.

Audio processing

DSP is unchanged from 0.2.1.

Reported test results

The supplied build/test report states that both platforms passed 11 automated suites, 360 audio/state comparisons and 32 lifecycle checks, plus installer checks. It reports that all 360 tested outputs matched 0.2.1 bit-for-bit on their respective platform. These results were not independently verified for this website.

Beta status

Hands-on testing in Bitwig, Live and Cubase is ongoing. This beta is not yet fully verified in those hosts; the automated results do not establish compatibility with every DAW or system.

Requirements & compatibility

Mac: Apple silicon M1 or later and macOS 13 or later. Windows: Windows 10 or 11 x64 with an x64 DAW. A VST3-compatible host is required. No standalone app, Intel Mac, native Windows ARM, AU or AAX version.

Licence

Closed-source freeware for personal and commercial audio use. Unmodified installers or complete bundles may be shared free of charge with the licence and third-party notices intact. See the included licence for the full terms.
